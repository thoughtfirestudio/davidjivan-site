ancient near eastern myth

Creation, chaos, and the sea

Chaoskampf
full entry
German term of art for the recurring ancient Near Eastern motif of a creator deity defeating a primordial chaos-monster.
Tiamat
full entry
Babylonian primordial goddess of the saltwater ocean; the cognate of Hebrew tehom.
Enuma Elish
full entry
Babylonian creation epic on seven tablets where Marduk slays Tiamat and forms the cosmos from her body.
Marduk
stub
Babylonian storm-god, patron of Babylon, and slayer of Tiamat in the Enuma Elish.
Tehom
stub
Hebrew word for the primordial deep in Genesis 1:2; demythologized cognate of Tiamat.
Leviathan
stub
Hebrew sea-serpent God smashes in Psalm 74 and Isaiah 27; a surviving trace of Chaoskampf in scripture.
Rahab
stub
Hebrew chaos-monster God cuts in pieces in Isaiah 51:9; named alongside Leviathan in Job 26 and Psalm 89.
Apsu
stub
Babylonian primordial god of fresh water and consort of Tiamat; killed by Ea before the cosmos is formed.
