Leviathan (Hebrew ×œÖ´×•Ö°×™Ö¸×ªÖ¸×Ÿ, Livyatan) is the sea-serpent of the Hebrew Bible — a chaos-monster of the deep that God subdues. In Psalm 74:14, God smashes Leviathan's heads in the waters. Isaiah 27:1 announces that the Lord will slay "Leviathan the fleeing serpent, Leviathan the twisting serpent." Job 41 describes the creature at length and in awe. Etymologically Leviathan is connected to the Ugaritic Lotan, the seven-headed sea-monster Baal defeats in the Baal Cycle — making him one of the clearest surviving traces of Chaoskampf in the Hebrew Bible, the older combat motif preserved in poetry and prophecy even after Genesis 1 demythologized it. Later Jewish and Christian apocalyptic literature took Leviathan up as an eschatological figure.
